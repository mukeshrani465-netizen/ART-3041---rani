Your commute is your vanity mirror.

New York doesn't get ready at home. It gets ready between stops: a swipe of balm before the doors open at Union Square, one last check in the dark window on the Q, a fix at the light on the Williamsburg Bridge.

So we made a balm for the ride. Five sheer, glossy tints, each named after a borough and coloured after its subway line. Find your line, or collect every stop.

Step into the Glossy Booth.

A subway photobooth that tries the balm on you. Pick your ride, stand clear of the doors, and watch your lips pick up your borough's tint live, frame after frame, stop after stop.

  1. Pick your ride. Your line, your From and your To, out of 139 real stations.
  2. Hold still. 3-2-1, flash. Each frame is a stop on your route. Tinted, clear shine or no balm at all.
  3. Take the strip home. Printed in lace, candy stripe, crochet or subway tile, plus a Your Line poster of your commute.
Open the booth → Your camera never leaves your device. No camera? Ride with the demo rider.
