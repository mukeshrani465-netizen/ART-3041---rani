# brand-skills.md (v1.0) — Glossier × NYC Subway: *Stand Clear of the Glossy Doors*

_Rulebook for the **Prompt Builder Tool** (`prompt-builder.html`). Every image prompt the tool writes must obey these rules; the choices in the tool only vary things *inside* them. Source: `creative-director-style-guide.html` (Pt. 1 Style Guide) and the Dynamic Hero prompt. Target image generator: **ChatGPT (GPT image)**; prompts are written in plain descriptive sentences, not keyword soup._

**The idea, in one line:** *Your commute is your vanity mirror.* The subway is where New York gets ready. The campaign borrows the MTA's visual language (line bullets, Helvetica signs, white tile, the yellow platform edge) and softens it with Glossier pink and dewy, skin-first imagery.

**Tone:** warm, witty, effortless. Glossier's "you" voice, said in MTA announcement style. Clean, dewy, real. Never glam, never gritty.

---

## 1. Brief
| | |
|---|---|
| Client | Glossier |
| Product | **Balm Dotcom Five Boroughs Set**: 5 limited tinted lip balms, one per borough |
| Market | New York City only |
| Audience | New Yorkers 18–34 who get ready on the train between home, work and class |
| Placements | Car cards, platform posters, station takeovers, billboards, bus shelters, wheat paste, building projections, phone reels |

## 2. The product (describe it the same way every time)
- **Balm Dotcom tube:** a soft, squeezable matte tube with a flat crimp at the end and a small round screw cap. The tube body is milky translucent white-pink; the tint colour shows through the tube and on the cap band. Glossy sheen on the balm itself, soft matte on the tube.
- **The set:** five tubes lined up in a row, in borough order (1, J, 7, 4, SI), in a slim Balm Pink sleeve box with a black station-sign band across the top.
- The balm on lips looks **sheer, glossy, hydrated**: a wash of colour, never opaque lipstick.
- Never invent other products. Never show competitor packaging.

## 3. The five borough tints
Each ad features **one tint as its hero colour**. Never more than two tints in one image (the "set" shot is the only exception: all five, in order).

| Tint | Borough | Line bullet | Bullet hex | Balm hex | Describe as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cherry Express** | Manhattan | 1 | #EE352E | #C8323A | sheer cherry red |
| **Cocoa Local** | Brooklyn | J | #996633 | #8A5A3C | warm cocoa brown |
| **Plum 7** | Queens | 7 | #B933AD | #8E2F7E | deep berry plum |
| **Mint Clear** | Bronx | 4 | #00933C | #CFE8D5 | clear balm with a cool mint tube |
| **Ferry Coral** | Staten Island | SI (ferry) | #FF6319 | #F26B4F | bright juicy coral |

The **line colour** is for the round bullet only; the **softer tint** is the actual balm.

## 4. Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Balm Pink | #F7CAD0 | backgrounds, packaging, light |
| Balm Pink deep | #E9A3AE | G-line logomark |
| Sign Black | #1A1A1A | headline bands, type |
| Tile White | #F5F1EA | paper, tile, page background |
| Steel Grey | #A7A9AC | rules, captions, metal, subway car steel |
| Platform Yellow | #FCCC0A | **accent only**: platform-edge stripe, "new" tag |

- **Colour ratio per image:** 45% Balm Pink · 25% Tile White · 20% Sign Black · 7% Steel · 3% Platform Yellow, plus the one hero tint.
- Light is soft and warm. Pink comes from light, walls, packaging and wardrobe, not from a filter.

## 5. Photography & image style
- **Look:** editorial beauty photography, natural and dewy. Real skin texture, visible pores, freckles welcome, minimal makeup, glossy lips, no heavy retouching.
- **Light (pick one per image):** soft morning window light on the train · golden hour through the elevated-line windows · overcast diffuse daylight on the platform · warm tungsten station light · rain on the window with soft city bokeh.
- **Camera:** 35mm or 50mm look, shallow depth of field, slight film grain, true-to-life colour.
- **People:** diverse New Yorkers 18–34, real and relatable, everyday clothes (tote bags, headphones, coats, school bags). Candid, getting ready on the go. Never posed glam models.
- **Settings:** inside a subway car (steel walls, orange/yellow seats, poles, sliding doors, window reflections) · station platform (white subway tile, mosaic station names, yellow platform edge) · elevated tracks (Queens 7 line, Bronx 4 line) · Staten Island Ferry deck · street-level station entrances (green globes).
- **Signature visual moves:** the window or the sliding door as a mirror · applying balm in a reflection · the tube held like a MetroCard · the five tubes lined up like a route map · tile mosaic letters.

## 6. Typography (for any text inside the image)
- **Headline / signage:** Inter Tight Bold (stand-in for MTA Helvetica). Sentence case, tight tracking, white on a Sign Black band with a thin white line above the text, like MTA station signs.
- **Body / captions:** Inter Tight Light / Regular.
- **Accent:** Instrument Serif, for **tint names and one word of emphasis only**, never full headlines.
- Keep text in images **short** (max ~8 words for the headline). Spell exact copy in quotes in the prompt.
- Approved copy lines:
  - "Stand clear of the glossy doors."
  - "Next stop: {tint name}"
  - "Five boroughs. Five tints. One balm."
  - "Your commute is your vanity mirror."
  - "Balm Dotcom Five Boroughs Set. Limited edition, NYC only."
  - Caption: "LIMITED EDITION · NYC ONLY"

## 7. Logomark: the G line
- Glossier's **G set as a subway bullet**: a perfect circle in Balm Pink deep (#E9A3AE) or Sign Black, white bold G.
- Collab lockup: `[G bullet] Glossier × NYC Subway`.
- Keep the bullet a perfect circle, clear space = half its width.
- Never stretch it, outline it, add shadows, or colour it Platform Yellow (reads as an N/Q/R/W train).
- On photos, the lockup sits on a **solid band**, never directly on the image.
- Note for prompts: image generators often draw logos badly. Ask for "a simple pink circle with a white bold letter G" and add the real logo in design software when exactness matters.

## 8. Image types the tool can write
1. **Product hero:** the tube or the set alone, studio-clean, on Balm Pink or tile.
2. **Lifestyle on the train:** a person applying balm on the subway.
3. **Close-up / detail:** lips, swatch, texture, tube in hand.
4. **Flat lay:** tube with commute items (MetroCard/OMNY phone, headphones, keys, coffee, tote).
5. **Station scene:** the product placed in a station (on a tile ledge, bench, turnstile).
6. **Print ad mockup:** a finished ad shown in place in NYC (see §9).
7. **Social / reel frame:** vertical, phone-camera feel, shot on the train.

## 9. Print ad placements (for mockups)
| Placement | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Subway car card | 11:4 wide (use 3:1) | above the seats inside the car |
| Platform poster | 2:3 portrait (46×60 in) | framed on white tile wall |
| Station takeover | 16:9 | stairs, columns and walls wrapped in the campaign |
| Billboard | 16:9 / 3:1 | e.g. Times Square, Queensboro Plaza, Atlantic Ave |
| Bus shelter | 2:3 portrait, backlit | street level, people waiting |
| Wheat paste | 4:5 | repeated posters on a construction wall, slightly wrinkled |
| Building projection | 16:9 | projected at night on a brick or stone facade |

The ad shown inside a mockup follows the layout: **black station-sign band on top with the headline**, product or person in the middle, line bullets in a row, small lockup bottom corner, thin yellow platform-edge stripe along the bottom.

## 10. Angles & framing
Eye level · low angle (looking up at the billboard / poster) · high angle / overhead (flat lay) · over-the-shoulder into a window reflection · close-up · extreme macro · wide establishing (whole platform or street) · three-quarter view · through the subway door window · mirror/reflection shot.

## 11. Hard rules (never break)
1. One hero tint per image (two max; all five only in the set shot, in order 1 · J · 7 · 4 · SI).
2. Balm Pink is the dominant colour; Platform Yellow is a thin accent only.
3. No gradients except the steel door sheen and the gloss highlight on the product.
4. Real, dewy skin. No heavy retouching, no plastic skin, no glam/red-carpet makeup, no opaque lipstick.
5. NYC is real and clean: no trash, no graffiti-dirt grime, no crime or danger mood, no fake subway lines or invented bullet colours.
6. Text in images is short and spelled exactly. Fonts: neo-grotesk (Helvetica / Inter Tight) for headlines, a soft serif only for tint names.
7. No other brands' logos, no competitor products, no real celebrities.
8. Every image must answer the idea: **your commute is your vanity mirror**.
9. Footer note for any public page: "A student project, not affiliated with Glossier or the MTA."

## 12. Negative prompt (always added)
`no heavy makeup, no plastic or airbrushed skin, no opaque lipstick, no gradients, no neon, no dark or gritty mood, no trash or dirt, no extra logos or brand names, no misspelled text, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no warped product, no invented subway line colours, no yellow G, no clutter`

## 13. Prompt formula (how the tool assembles a prompt)
`[Image type sentence] + [subject & product, with hero tint] + [setting] + [angle & framing] + [light & mood] + [colour palette & ratio] + [text in image, exact copy] + [style & camera] + [brand rules] + [negative prompt] + [aspect ratio]`

## 14. Self-check before using an image
- Does Balm Pink dominate, with one hero tint?
- Is the product recognisable as a Balm Dotcom tube?
- Is the skin real and dewy, the lips sheer and glossy?
- Is it clearly NYC transit (tile, steel, bullets, platform edge)?
- Is all text spelled right and short?
- Would it make a New Yorker smile on the way to work?
