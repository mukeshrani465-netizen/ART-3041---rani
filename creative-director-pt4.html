Creative Director Pt. 4: Print Ads

G Glossier × NYC Subway — Stand Clear of the Glossy Doors

Five print ads for the Balm Dotcom Five Boroughs Set. One campaign idea, your commute is your vanity mirror, told five ways: each ad has its own concept, headline and borough tint, placed where New Yorkers actually see ads on their way to work.

Process

  1. Inspiration: MTA signage and service notices, Vignelli's 1972 subway map, and Glossier's own pink, skin-first campaigns.
  2. Placement first: each ad was planned for a real spot in the system (platform wall, stairway, column, car interior, car exterior), which set its format before anything was generated.
  3. Prompts: written with my prompt builder from the brand rules (palette, typography, the G-line logo, one tint per ad), with a shared setup message so all five ads read as one shoot: the same model, light and signature.
  4. Generation: each ad was generated as flat artwork first, not mocked up, to keep it sharp and consistent.
  5. Mockups: the flat ads were then placed into NYC subway scenes with AI.
