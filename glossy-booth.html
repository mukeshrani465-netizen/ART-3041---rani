The Glossy Booth

G Glossier × NYC Subway — Stand Clear of the Glossy Doors

A subway photobooth where you are the campaign model. Pick your commute, step through the glossy doors, and the booth tints your lips in your borough's balm, live (or just adds a clear shine, or nothing at all). It shoots a strip, prints it in the frame you choose, and turns your ride into a poster. Every visitor makes a different ad. Your camera never leaves your device.

Your Line, Your Poster

Your commute, set as a campaign poster. Each press of Next poster mixes a new layout, headline and tint from your ride, so the stream never ends.

Five lines, five boroughs, 139 stations: 3,914 possible rides, each with six layouts and a dozen headlines.

The platform wall

Strips and posters pinned in this browser. Booth strips stay on your device.

How it works

  1. Face tracking: Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker runs in the browser and finds 478 points on the face. The booth uses the 40 points around the lips to draw a mask.
  2. The tint: the lip mask is filled with the borough balm (multiply plus soft light) and given a gloss highlight. In B&W film only the lips stay in color, like a hand-tinted booth print.
  3. The strip: each frame is a stop on your real line: the station sign above the window changes as you ride. The footer draws your route Vignelli-style and stamps the ride like a MetroCard.
  4. The prints: five frames drawn in code (subway strip, lace and rhinestones, candy stripes, crochet, tile and steel), inspired by how people frame and decorate real booth strips.
  5. The posters: a generator in the spirit of my earlier poster generator: ride data + brand rules + six layouts + a headline bank = an endless series.
